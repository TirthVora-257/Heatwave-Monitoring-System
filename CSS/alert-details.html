[RED ALERT] Extreme Heatwave in Effect

High temperature anomalies exceeding 45°C detected in central and western zones. Vulnerable populations must avoid direct outdoor exposure between 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM.

Heatwave Severity Categories

  • Green (Normal): Maximum temperatures within normal seasonal limits. No advisory required.
  • Yellow (Watch): Moderate heat conditions; high temperatures persist for 2 consecutive days.
  • Orange (Alert): Severe heat alert; temperatures 40°C to 45°C. Vulnerable groups must stay hydrated.
  • Red (Warning): Extreme heat emergency; temperatures exceeding 45°C. High risk of heatstroke.

Public Safety Guidelines

  • Drink adequate water and oral rehydration solutions (ORS) at regular intervals.
  • Wear lightweight, loose-fitting, and light-colored cotton clothing.
  • Never leave children or pets in parked vehicles.
  • Recognize signs of heatstroke: dizziness, nausea, high fever, and confusion.
