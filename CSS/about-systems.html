System Objective

The primary objective of this platform is to deliver real-time heatwave risk tracking and automated early warnings. By integrating high-resolution environmental monitoring with predictive analytics, the system helps minimize public health risks during critical heat events.

Message from Leadership

"Timely intelligence transforms potential climate disasters into manageable safety protocols. Our automated monitoring ensures every vulnerable region receives early warnings before severe ambient thresholds are breached."

— Chief Meteorologist, DMC

Data Sources & Telemetry

System analytics rely on multi-node telemetry combining ground observations from AWS arrays with regional updates from the IMD. Thermal metrics are continuously refreshed to monitor localized surges exceeding standard HW and SHW thresholds above 47°C(116.6°F).

Key Stakeholders

Alert broadcasts are distributed directly to primary response networks:

  • Municipal Disaster Management Authorities — Emergency resource deployment.
  • Healthcare Infrastructure — Heatstroke emergency triage readiness.
  • Public Information Outlets — Broadcasting community safety advisories.
