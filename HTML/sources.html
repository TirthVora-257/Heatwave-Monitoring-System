Data Sources & Stakeholder Roles Summer 2026

Forecast & Alert Workflow Steps
  1. Data Ingestion: Continuous telemetry collection from ground stations and satellite networks.
  2. Threshold Analysis: Automated evaluation against predefined ambient temperature limits.
  3. Alert Generation: Immediate categorization into standard or severe heatwave alert levels.
  4. Broadcast & Distribution: Real-time dispatch to emergency response units and public channels.

Primary Data Sources
  • Automatic Weather Station (AWS) Sensors: Surface temperature, humidity, and atmospheric pressure tracking.
  • IMD Database Integration: Direct connection to historical climate datasets and regional forecast models.
  • Geostationary Satellite Feeds: Thermal infrared imagery for detecting urban heat island effects.

Stakeholders & Operational Actions
  • Disaster Management Authorities
    • Deploy mobile water distribution tankers to critical zones.
    • Issue public cooling center operational orders.
  • Healthcare Infrastructure
    • Prepare emergency heatstroke isolation wards.
    • Stock essential intravenous fluids and oral rehydration supplies.
  • Public Media & Broadcast Networks
    • Interrupt regular programming with urgent heat warning banners.
    • Publish daily advisory precautions for vulnerable demographics.

Technical Abbreviations Glossary
AWS
Automatic Weather Station — Automated surface telemetry units providing real-time local observations.

IMD
India Meteorological Department — The principal government agency responsible for meteorological observations and forecasting.

HW
Heatwave — A period of abnormally hot weather exceeding location-specific threshold temperatures.

SHW
Severe Heatwave — An extreme climate event marked by dangerous temperature spikes significantly above normal limits.
